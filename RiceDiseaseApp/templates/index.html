🌾 Rice Leaf Disease Detection and Solution System

The Rice Leaf Disease Detection System is a smart web application designed to help farmers and researchers identify common diseases affecting rice plants. It uses advanced Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) to analyze the uploaded image of a rice leaf and automatically detect whether the leaf is Healthy or affected by a specific disease. The system also recommends the appropriate chemical treatment, water management tips, and farming advice based on the detected disease.

🔍 How It Works

The system uses a pre-trained Deep Learning model that can identify and classify rice leaf diseases by examining the color, texture, and pattern of the uploaded leaf image. Once a user uploads an image, the system processes it, performs feature extraction, and predicts the disease type with high accuracy.

🌿 Diseases It Can Detect

  • Healthy: Leaf is normal, no signs of infection.
  • Brown Spot: Caused by fungus Bipolaris oryzae, appears as brown lesions on leaves.
  • Leaf Blast: Caused by fungus Magnaporthe oryzae, shows diamond-shaped grayish spots.
  • Leaf Blight: Bacterial infection caused by Xanthomonas oryzae, causes yellowing and drying of leaves.

⚙️ Process Flow

  1. Step 1: Collect images of rice leaves (Healthy, Brown Spot, Leaf Blast, Leaf Blight).
  2. Step 2: Train the CNN and VGG16 models using these images.
  3. Step 3: Upload a new rice leaf image from the field.
  4. Step 4: The trained model analyzes the image and identifies the disease.
  5. Step 5: The system displays:
    • ✅ Disease Name
    • ✅ Stage of Disease (Early / Moderate / Severe)
    • ✅ Chemical Solution
    • ✅ Water Suggestion
    • ✅ Helpful Farming Advice
